Research notesWorking hypothesis

A question I keep coming back to: whatever an LLM does is still driven by the next token - no external state changes that. But can we hold a state on top of what the LLM already does - an internal model of what it knows and doesn't know, an epistemic state - and use that abstraction to drive the LLM's behavior? I suspect durable reasoning, emotion and creativity live in that layer around the model, not inside it. Early signals and reading notes, not findings - the thinking-in-progress shows up in my essays.
